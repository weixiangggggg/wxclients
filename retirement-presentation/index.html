OFFICIAL GUIDANCE

Sources & assumptions

A few helpful places to check the details for your own situation. Rules and estimates can change, so use current official guidance.

  • CPF Board — CPF LIFE ↗

    Plan features, payout timing, deferral and what may be distributed to beneficiaries.

  • CPF Board — Monthly Payout Estimator ↗

    Estimate CPF payouts using your own balances and intended payout start age.

  • CPF Board — Account services and nominations ↗

    Find current guidance on CPF nominations and distributing CPF savings.

  • MoneySense Singapore ↗

    Official financial education on planning, investments and risk.

  • Monetary Authority of Singapore ↗

    Singapore’s central bank and financial regulator.

  • Supplied reference: Golden Year Partners retirement brochure
